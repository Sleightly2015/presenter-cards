# Why sound matters
@ 2:00
- Audiences forgive soft images. They leave over bad audio.
- "Sound is half the experience." *George Lucas*
> Play clip A (bad), then clip B (fixed)

# The one rule: distance
@ 5:00
1. Get the mic close to the mouth
2. Then reduce noise in the room
3. Only then reach for software
> Demo: phone at 2 m vs. 20 cm

# Your phone is a recorder
@ 9:00
- Airplane mode, so no notification beeps
- Record in WAV if your app allows it
- Earbuds with a mic: clip near the collar, cable away from clothes
- Record 30 seconds of room tone before every scene

# Room fixes that cost nothing
@ 13:00
- Fridge, AC, laptop fans: off or out of the room
- Soft rooms beat hard rooms: sofa, curtains, a wardrobe full of clothes
- Avoid corners and bare walls; turn so the speaker faces into the softest part of the room
- Listen for traffic, planes, neighbours and church bells, and plan takes around them
- Wear headphones while recording: if you can't hear a problem, you can't fix it
